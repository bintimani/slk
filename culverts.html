What We Build

Four Types of Work

Box Culverts

Reinforced concrete box sections for high-load road crossings. Built to handle heavy vehicles and peak rainy season water flow.

Pipe Culverts

Concrete or metal pipe for smaller drainage channels. Fast to install and cost-effective for farm tracks and residential roads.

Small Footbridges

Concrete or steel-reinforced bridges up to ~10 metres. Connecting communities cut off by streams every rainy season.

Drainage & Headwalls

Inlet and outlet headwalls, wing walls, and channel lining to stop erosion and protect the culvert long-term.

Why It Matters

Sierra Leone's drainage problem is everywhere

Over 88% of Sierra Leone's roads remain unpaved. Every rainy season, streams cut off communities, destroy road surfaces, and damage property — not because the rain is unusual, but because the drainage was never built.

Done properly, a culvert lasts decades and keeps access open year-round. Skip it, and you're rebuilding the road every year.
