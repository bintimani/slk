FIG. 03 Why It Matters

Sierra Leone's drainage problem is everywhere.

Every rainy season, streams cut off communities, destroy road surfaces and damage property — not because the rain is unusual, but because the drainage was never built.

Done properly, a culvert lasts decades and keeps access open year-round. Skip it, and you're rebuilding the road every year.

88% of Sierra Leone's roads remain unpaved — drainage is the single biggest reason culverts get built. source · public infrastructure data
